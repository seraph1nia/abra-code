# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Discover contextual planning reviews

The app SHALL expose published reviews in their producing conversation and alongside the corresponding planning document or generated surface. Associations SHALL use retained native identities. Observation and navigation MUST NOT submit feedback or change work state.

#### Scenario: Open a plan with a review

- **WHEN** a user opens a planning document with a published review
- **THEN** the exact review is available beside the document and shares its draft with the conversation presentation
